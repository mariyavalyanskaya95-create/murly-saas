Plan your farm warm-up

Choose a season. Plant crops in your 6 fields. One field = a few, two fields = some, three or more = a lot of. Then read your plan out loud!

Farm words vocabulary

Click a card: listen, read the example and look at the label. C = countable (you can count it: one egg, two eggs). U = uncountable (some milk, a bottle of milk).

Нотатка: на звороті кожної картки є позначка countable або uncountable. Спитайте: «Can you count it? One milk, two milks?» Хай учень сам здогадається, чому так не можна.

Countable or uncountable? grammar

Some things you can count: one carrot, two carrots. Some things you can't: milk, flour, honey. The words we use with them are different!

But: when you ask for something or offer something, use some: Can I have some water? Would you like some cheese?

How much is it?

  • a lot of: with both! a lot of eggs, a lot of milk
  • many / a few: only countable
  • much / a little: only uncountable
  • In the shop: How much is the honey? How much are the pumpkins? (= the price)

Quick check +5 XP each

Answers: 1 many · 2 much · 3 some · 4 any · 5 a few · 6 a little · 7 a lot of · 8 any

Нотатка: в українській «хліб», «гроші», «мед» теж без множини, а «яблуко» чи «морквина» рахуються так само, як в англійській. Пастки: bread, money, fruit, advice, information не мають множини. Some у стверджувальних реченнях і в проханнях (Can I have some...?), any в запереченнях і питаннях.

Click a word, then click the right crate. Can you count it?

Countable a / an · many · a few

Uncountable much · a little

Countable: egg, carrot, apple, potato, coin, sheep, seed, tomato, cow. Uncountable: milk, flour, honey, cheese, water, money, bread, wool, sugar.

Нотатка: якщо учень помиляється з bread чи money, поверніться до правила й покажіть «a loaf of bread», «three coins». Sheep: одна вівця і багато овець, слово не змінюється, але воно злічуване.
