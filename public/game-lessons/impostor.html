Spaceship Mystery

What were you doing when the lights went out? · Past Continuous · Level B1 · 60 min

LEVEL 1
0 XP 0

In this lesson you will:

  • Rate yourself as a detective and talk about your memory.
  • Learn the rooms and tasks on a spaceship.
  • Read four crew logs and find out who was where.
  • Learn the Past Continuous: was / were + -ing, and when / while.
  • Practise in an emergency meeting dialogue.
  • Solve the mystery: who ate the captain's birthday cake?
  • Check alibis in pairs and record your own case report.
  • Revise the key phrases with flashcards.

1Can you relate? warm-uppairs

Read the statements. Choose a number from 1 to 5. 1 = Not me at all. 5 = That's totally me. Then compare with your partner and explain your choices.

Useful phrases
  • I'd give myself a ... because ...
  • I'm (not) very good at ...
  • I usually notice / forget ...
  • Me too! / Really? I'm the opposite.
