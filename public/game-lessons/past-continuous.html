2Reading: the great cookie mystery readingteacher + student

The great cookie mystery

Yesterday Mum baked 12 chocolate cookies. At 8 pm she looked at the plate. It was empty! “OK, everyone. What were you doing at 8 pm?” she asked.

Dad: “I was washing the car. Look, my hands are wet!”

Sam (15): “I was doing my homework. I wasn't eating anything, I promise!”

Grandma: “I was talking to my friend on the phone. We were laughing a lot.”

Little Leo (6): “I was sleeping!” (But his mouth was full of chocolate.)

And Pixel the cat? Pixel was sitting on the sofa. He was looking very happy. So who ate the cookies? Leo wasn't sleeping, that's for sure!

True or False?

1Mum baked 12 cookies.
2At 8 pm Dad was washing the car.
3Sam was eating cookies.
4Grandma was talking on the phone.
5Pixel was sleeping in the kitchen.
6Leo was really sleeping.

Переклад тексту: Учора мама спекла 12 шоколадних печеньок. О 8 вечора вона глянула на тарілку: порожня! «Отже, що ви всі робили о 8 вечора?» спитала вона. Тато: «Я мив машину. Дивись, у мене мокрі руки!» Сем (15): «Я робив домашку. Я нічого не їв, обіцяю!» Бабуся: «Я говорила з подругою по телефону. Ми багато сміялися.» Маленький Лео (6): «Я спав!» (Але його рот був повний шоколаду.) А кіт Піксель? Піксель сидів на дивані й виглядав дуже щасливим. То хто з'їв печиво? Лео точно не спав!

Translate from the text

2Я нічого не їв.
3Ми багато сміялися.
4Кіт сидів на дивані.
5Що ви робили о 8 вечора?

Як читати: учень читає репліки «акторськими» голосами. Потім: «Who ate the cookies? How do you know?» (Leo! His mouth was full of chocolate.) Попросіть знайти всі was / were + -ing.
Відповіді: T, T, F, T, F, F.

4The rule: a video on pause grammarteacher + student

Past Continuous = an action in progress at a moment in the past. Imagine you pressed pause on a video: at 8 pm I was doing my homework.

Form

was / were + -ing

I / he / she / it was watching.
you / we / they were playing.

Negative and questions

wasn't / weren't

I wasn't sleeping.
Was she studying? Yes, she was.
What were you doing at 10?

Signal words

at 8 pm yesterday, all evening, at that moment

At this time yesterday we were swimming.

Photo or video? Past Simple is a photo: it happened, finished. Past Continuous is a video on pause: the action was going on.

Як пояснити: порівняйте з Present Continuous: am / is / are + -ing → was / were + -ing. «Yesterday at 9 pm you were... what?»
Правопис -ing: make → making, swim → swimming, run → running, lie → lying.

6Picture time: what were they doing? speakingteacher + student

Someone took these photos yesterday at 5 pm. What were the people doing at that moment? Say 2-3 sentences about each photo.

Write the verb

1Photo 1: The girls ___ (play) volleyball on the beach.
2Photo 2: The friends ___ (jump) at sunset.
3Photo 3: Three people ___ (walk) in the rain.
4Photo 4: The women ___ (dance) at a party.
5Photo 5: The boys ___ (laugh) and playing.
6Photo 6: Two girls ___ (look) at a phone.
Say more
  • It was sunny / raining / evening.
  • They weren't ... They were ...
  • I think they were happy because ...
  • One girl was wearing ...

Зразок до фото 1: «It was a sunny day. Three girls were playing volleyball on the beach. One girl was jumping. They weren't swimming. They were having fun!»
Навідні питання: Where were they? What was the weather like? What were they wearing? Were they happy? What was the girl in the middle doing?
Слова на допомогу: umbrella (парасоля), sunset (захід сонця), beach (пляж), wearing (одягнений у).
