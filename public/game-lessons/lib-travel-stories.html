3Reading readingteacher + student

A trip I will never forget

Last summer my family went to the Rocky Mountains. We stayed in a small wooden house near a forest. On the first day it was raining all the time, so we played board games and drank hot tea.

On the second day the sun was shining, and we set off early. We were walking near a lake when we saw a bear. It was drinking water and didn’t notice us. Nobody said a word. We stood very still, then we quietly walked back to the path.

My dad was so excited that he took a photo, but his hands were shaking, so it was blurry! Later, while we were having dinner, we told the owner of the house about the bear. He laughed and said, "Luckily, bears are usually more afraid of people than people are of bears."

Now, every time I see that blurry photo, I remember the most exciting day of my summer.

4Grammar grammarteacher + student

Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing) описує фон, тобто що тривало: The sun was shining. Past Simple описує подію, яка цей фон перервала: We were walking to the hotel when it started to rain. Після while зазвичай іде Past Continuous: While we were having dinner, the phone rang.

Put the verbs in Past Simple or Past Continuous.

1We ___ (walk) near a lake when we ___ (see) a bear.
2The bear ___ (drink) water.
3It ___ (not notice) us.
4While I ___ (take) a photo, my sister ___ (shout).

Відповіді: were walking / saw · was drinking · didn’t notice · was taking / shouted

Choose the correct option.

1When the phone rang, I ___ a shower.
2We ___ lost on the first day.
3While we ___ for the bus, it started to snow.

Відповіді: was having · got · were waiting

2Поки мама купувала квитки, ми чекали в кафе

Відповіді: We were travelling by train when I saw my teacher · While Mum was buying tickets, we were waiting in a cafe

Намалюй часову лінію: довга хвиляста лінія (Past Continuous) і коротка «блискавка» (Past Simple), що її перериває. Попроси учня придумати власне речення за цією схемою. Переклад може мати кілька правильних варіантів: якщо відповідь логічна, зарахуй її вручну. 10 хвилин.

5Listening and speaking listeningteacher + student

A trip that went wrong

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

Anna: How was your trip to York, Josh?

Josh: Well, it started badly. We missed our train!

Anna: Oh no! What happened?

Josh: We were sitting in a cafe at the station and we were talking so much that we didn’t hear the announcement.

Anna: So what did you do?

Josh: Luckily, there was another train two hours later. While we were waiting, we walked around and found a great bookshop.

Anna: And how was York?

Josh: Amazing! But on the second day it was raining when we set off, and I forgot my umbrella at the hotel.

Anna: Typical you! Did you buy any souvenirs?

Josh: Yes, I bought some chocolate for you. But I ate half of it on the train back. Sorry!

Listen. True or False?

1Josh missed his train because he was late in the morning.
2They took another train two hours later.
3Josh found a bookshop while he was waiting.
4Josh brought all the chocolate back for Anna.

Відповіді: False · True · True · False

Tell about a trip you remember. Where were you? What were you doing when something happened?
Have you ever got lost? What did you do?
Have you ever missed a train, a bus or a plane?
Story chain: start with "It was a dark and rainy night. We were driving through the forest when…" and continue together.

Аудіо прослухати двічі. Потім гра «Story chain»: вчитель і учень по черзі додають по одному реченню до історії, використовуючи when / while / suddenly / luckily. Далі учень розповідає власну історію 1-2 хвилини. Виправляй тільки помилки з минулими часами, повторюючи фразу правильно. 14 хвилин.

Можна використати як розминку на наступному уроці. Перед першою секцією намалюй ще раз часову лінію: довга дія (was/were + -ing) і коротка подія (Past Simple). У перекладі зараховуються різні правильні варіанти (saw / noticed, amazing / beautiful), інші логічні відповіді можна зарахувати вручну. Після гри попроси учня продовжити 2-3 речення власними кінцівками.
