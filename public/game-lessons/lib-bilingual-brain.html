5Speaking speakingteacher + student

Mini-debate. Read the statement. Choose a side, prepare two arguments and one example in 2 minutes, then present your opinion. Use the phrases from the tip.

Which language would you like to learn next? Why?
Agree or disagree: "Everyone in Ukraine should speak English." Give two reasons.
Do you think online translators will make learning languages unnecessary?
Look at your answers on the scale from the warm-up. Has your opinion changed after the video?

Record a 1-minute opinion.

"Every child should learn two foreign languages at school." Do you agree? Give two arguments and one example from the video or your life. (say it out loud)

Міні-дебати: вчитель бере протилежну позицію, учень захищає свою. Кожна сторона говорить 1 хвилину, потім 1 хвилина на відповідь. Слідкуй за використанням фраз One advantage of…, On the other hand… Помилки записуй і розбирай після дебатів, а не під час. 14 хвилин.
