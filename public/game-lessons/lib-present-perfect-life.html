1Warm-up warm-upteacher + student

Two truths and a lie. Your teacher says three sentences about their life. Two are true, one is false. Can you guess the lie?

Guess the lie: which sentence is not true?
Now say three sentences about your life: two true, one false.

Дай свої три речення з Present Perfect: I have been to Wales. I have never eaten sushi. I have met a famous singer. Учень вгадує брехню і ставить питання. Потім учень робить свої три речення. Не пояснюй граматику, просто познач, що всі речення про досвід «колись у житті». 5 хвилин.

3Reading · НМТ readingteacher + student

"I have never said no to a new experience"

A Seventeen-year-old Emma from Oxford has a simple rule: if something new scares her a little, she tries it. "I have climbed a mountain, I have performed on stage and I have even tried haggis in Scotland," she laughs.

B Her biggest adventure so far happened two years ago. She went to a summer camp abroad and didn’t know anyone there. "On the first day I was terrified. But by the end of the camp I had friends from six countries. We still chat every week."

C Not everything has been easy. Emma has failed a few times. "I tried skateboarding last spring and broke my arm. I haven’t got on a skateboard since then!" But she says mistakes are part of the fun.

D Recently Emma has started a blog about her experiences. She has already written twenty posts, and more than a thousand people have read them. She hasn’t decided what to study at university yet, but she knows one thing: "I want a life full of stories."

Формат як у НМТ: спершу заголовки до абзаців (на кожен абзац 30-40 секунд, шукати ключові слова), потім завдання на деталі. Після перевірки знайдіть у тексті приклади Present Perfect і Past Simple та поясніть, чому вжито саме цей час (two years ago, last spring - точний час). 15 хвилин.

4Grammar grammarteacher + student

Present Perfect (have / has + V3) говорить про досвід без точного часу: I have been to York. Якщо є точний час (yesterday, in 2022, two years ago), потрібен Past Simple: I went to York in 2022. Типовий діалог: Have you ever been to York? Yes, I have. When did you go there? I went last summer.

4We ___ (know) each other since 2019.

Останнє завдання - у форматі граматичного тесту НМТ (вибір з чотирьох варіантів у зв’язному тексті). Попроси учня спершу знайти в кожному реченні «маркер часу» (so far, last year, never, just) і лише потім обирати. Помилки розбирай через питання: Is there a finished time? 12 хвилин.

5Listening and speaking listeningteacher + student

Interview: the experience game

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

Kate: OK, my first question. Have you ever been on TV?

Oliver: Yes, I have, actually!

Kate: Really? When was that?

Oliver: Two years ago. A journalist interviewed me at a football match. I was so nervous!

Kate: Cool! Have you ever cooked a meal for your whole family?

Oliver: No, I haven’t. I’ve only made sandwiches. What about you? Have you ever broken a bone?

Kate: Yes, I’ve broken my leg. I fell off a horse when I was ten.

Oliver: Ouch! Have you been on a horse since then?

Kate: No, never. I’ve been too scared!

Listen. True or False?

1Oliver has been on TV.
2Oliver has cooked dinner for his family many times.
3Kate broke her leg when she was ten.

Відповіді: True · False · True

Have you ever met a famous person?
What is the most interesting place you have been to? When did you go there?
What have you done this week that you are proud of?
Find someone who…: ask your teacher five Have you ever…? questions and one follow-up question in Past Simple for each Yes.

НМТ-style speaking practice: record a 1-minute answer.

Talk about an experience that changed you. What have you done? When did it happen? How did you feel? (say it out loud)

Після аудіо зверни увагу на модель: питання в Present Perfect, а уточнення (When? How?) уже в Past Simple. Гра «Find someone who…»: учень ставить питання з картки (…has eaten something strange, …has been abroad, …has met a famous person), на кожне Yes - уточнення в Past Simple. Потім запис голосом. 12 хвилин.

Third forms

Назви третю форму дієслова (V3).

Click "Next card"

Відповіді: gone · been · seen · eaten · written · broken · bought · met · tried · known

Формат як у НМТ: перша і третя секції - вибір з чотирьох варіантів. Навчи стратегію: спершу знайти маркер часу (since, for, last month, in 2015, so far, yet), потім обирати. Неочевидні місця: «She has worked… and she still loves» - дія триває досі, тому Present Perfect; «Since then she has been abroad» - since then вимагає Present Perfect; «Has she ever been» - після has потрібна V3. У грі з V3 учень спершу відповідає усно, потім складає 2-3 речення з I have… Можна використати як розминку на наступному уроці.
