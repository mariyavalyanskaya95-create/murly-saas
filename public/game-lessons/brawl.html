In this lesson you will:

  • Create your own brawler card and say what it can and can't do.
  • Learn team words: roles, the map and the objective.
  • Learn can / can't, must / mustn't and should / shouldn't.
  • Sort game rules, tips and abilities.
  • Play GEM GRAB: choose the right modal and collect ten gems for your team.
  • Read a message from a team captain and fix grammar bugs.
  • Talk about games and write a beginner's guide.

Trap! After can, must and should: verb without to and without -s. She cans hide. → She can hide. · You must to win. → You must win. · Do you can heal? → Can you heal?

Quick check

Нотатка: can = вміння або дозвіл, must = правило чи сильний обов’язок, should = порада. Головні помилки: «must to», «she cans», «Do you can?». Після модальних дієслів завжди перша форма дієслова без to.

        6A message from the captain readingindividual

        Leo is new in the team. The captain, Zip, sends him a message. Read it. Then decide: True or False?

        Hi Leo! Welcome to the Yellow Stars, our team. Here are my tips for your first Gem Grab matches.

        First, the rules. In Gem Grab, gems appear in the middle of the map. Your team must collect ten gems. Then a countdown starts, and you must keep the gems until it ends. If the gem carrier's health goes to zero, the gems fall on the floor, and the other team can take them.

        Now the roles. Mo is our tank. He can take a lot of hits, so he should go first. Ana is our healer. She can't hit very hard, but she can heal us. I am the sniper. I can hit from far away, but I shouldn't stand too close to the other team.

        And you? You can be our gem carrier! You should stay behind Mo and pick up the gems. You shouldn't run into the middle alone. When you have a lot of gems, you should go back and hide in a bush.

        Last thing: we mustn't be rude in the chat. We win together and we lose together. Good luck and have fun!
        Zip

        Answers: 1 True · 2 False (ten gems) · 3 True · 4 False (she can't hit very hard) · 5 False (he shouldn't) · 6 True · 7 False (Zip is the sniper)

        Нотатка: перше читання на 2 хвилини: хто яку роль грає. Друге: знайти всі can, must, should і пояснити, це правило, порада чи вміння. Кнопка «Show the modals» підсвічує їх.
