4Listening listeningteacher + student

Max shows a photo

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

Anna: Max, is this your family?

Max: Yes, it is! This is my mum and this is my dad.

Anna: Who is this girl?

Max: This is my sister. Her name is Lily. She is ten.

Anna: She has got long hair.

Max: Yes, she has. And this is my grandma and grandpa. They live with us.

Anna: Have you got a brother?

Max: No, I haven’t. But I have got a cousin. His name is Tom.

Anna: Has Tom got a pet?

Max: Yes, he has. He has got a funny dog called Biscuit.

Anna: I love dogs! Have you got a pet, Max?

Max: No, I haven’t. But I want a cat!

Перед слуханням: Max shows his family photo. Who can you see? Перше слухання для загального розуміння, друге для деталей. Під час слухання дитина піднімає руку, коли чує слово про сім’ю. Можна читати діалог по ролях після вправ.

5Grammar: have got / has got grammarteacher + student

I / you / we / they have got, але he / she / it has got.

I have got a sister. She has got a cat.

Питання: Have you got a brother? Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t.

Has she got a pet? Yes, she has. / No, she hasn’t.

Гра «Family cards»: на картках різні члени сім’ї і тварини. Дитина тягне картку і каже: I have got a… Потім про тебе: You have got… І про ляльку чи іграшку: She has got… Часта помилка: She have got. Виправляй, показуючи жестом «s» у повітрі.

Встав have got або has got.

2Lily ___ long hair.
3We ___ a cat.
4My grandpa ___ a red car.
5___ you got a sister? Yes, I have.

Відповіді: have got · has got · have got · has got · Have

6Speaking speakingteacher + student

Who is in your family?
Have you got a brother or a sister?
Have you got a grandma? Where does she live?
Have you got a pet? What is its name?

Дитина показує фото чи малюнок і презентує сім’ю: This is my… She has got… Ти ставиш уточнювальні питання. Потім міняєтеся: ти описуєш вигадану сім’ю, дитина малює те, що чує. Наприкінці голосовий запис.

Запиши голосом.

Show your family photo or picture and tell about your family. Who is this? What has he or she got? (say it out loud)

7Writing writingteacher + student

Read Max’s text: Hello! I’m Max. I’m eight. I have got a big family. I have got one sister. Her name is Lily. She has got long hair. My grandma and grandpa live with us. My cousin Tom has got a funny dog called Biscuit.

Напиши про свою сім’ю (4-5 речень), як Макс.

Hello! I’m... I have got... My mum... My dad... I have got / I haven’t got a pet.

Exit task: назви 5 членів сім’ї за 20 секунд. Домашнє завдання: намалювати сімейне дерево (family tree) і підписати кожного: This is my grandma. She has got a garden.
