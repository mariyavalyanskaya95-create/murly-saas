1Warm-up warm-upteacher + student

Small talk is a short, friendly conversation about safe topics: the weather, the weekend, travel or plans. It helps people feel relaxed before a meeting and makes a new team feel less like strangers.

3Listening listeningteacher + student

Before you listen: Olivia has just started a new job. On Monday morning she meets a colleague in the office kitchen. What do you think they will talk about?

Monday morning in the office kitchen

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

Mark: Morning! I don’t think we’ve met. I’m Mark, from the sales team.

Olivia: Hi Mark, nice to meet you. I’m Olivia. I started in marketing last week.

Mark: Oh, welcome! How’s it going so far?

Olivia: Pretty well, thanks. Everyone is very friendly. It’s a bit busy, though.

Mark: Yes, Mondays are always crazy here. Did you have a good weekend?

Olivia: Yes, lovely, thanks. I went cycling by the river with my sister. How about you?

Mark: Nothing special. I watched football and cooked a big dinner for some friends.

Olivia: Sounds nice. What did you cook?

Mark: A Thai curry. It was a bit too spicy, but everyone ate it!

Olivia: Ha, that’s the main thing. By the way, is the coffee machine always this slow?

Mark: Always. That’s why we have the best conversations here.

Olivia: Good to know. Any plans for the summer holidays?

Mark: We’re thinking about Cornwall. Have you ever been there?

Olivia: No, never, but I’d love to go. Oh, my meeting starts in two minutes. I’d better get going.

Mark: Sure. It was nice talking to you. Let’s catch up at lunch sometime.

Olivia: Great idea. See you later!

Gist: listen once and choose the answer.

1Where are Olivia and Mark? ___
2Why does the conversation end? ___

Відповідай не одним словом, а коротким реченням і поверни питання: Not bad, thanks. I went hiking. How about you? Про минулі вихідні питаємо в Past Simple: Did you have…? / What did you do? / How was…? Про досвід взагалі в Present Perfect: Have you ever been to…?

Нагадайте різницю: конкретний час у минулому (weekend, Saturday) = Past Simple; досвід без часу = Present Perfect. Типова помилка українців: «How was your weekend? - Was good.» Виправляйте одразу: It was good. Вправи 6-7 хв, перевірка вголос.

5Speaking speakingteacher + student

Card A: the new colleague. You started at the company two weeks ago. You meet a colleague by the coffee machine. Introduce yourself, ask about their weekend and one more safe topic. Then end the conversation politely.

Card B: the experienced colleague. You have worked here for three years. Welcome the new colleague, answer with full sentences and ask questions back. Recommend a good place for lunch near the office.

Role play: use your cards. Speak for 2-3 minutes, then swap roles.
Which topics are not good for small talk? Are they the same in Ukraine and in the UK or the USA?
Is small talk a waste of time or an important work skill? Why?

Record a 1-minute answer.

A colleague asks you: "Did you have a good weekend?" Answer with two or three details and ask two questions back. (say it out loud)

Рольова гра в парах 2-3 хв на роль, потім обмін. В індивідуальному уроці вчитель грає роль B. Слухайте, чи учень повертає питання і чи завершує розмову ввічливо. Після гри дайте 2 сильні фрази і 2 виправлення. Voice можна дати вдома, якщо бракує часу.
