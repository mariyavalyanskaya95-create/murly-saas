3Past Simple: quick review grammarteacher + student

Read the rules. Then tell me three things you did yesterday: one positive, one negative, one question for me.

Regular verbs

verb + -ed

celebrate → celebrated · wrap → wrapped
decorate → decorated · try → tried · enjoy → enjoyed

Irregular verbs

their own form

give → gave · make → made · hang → hung · hide → hid · freeze → froze · steal → stole

Negative

didn't + verb

Santa didn't come. (NOT "didn't came")

Questions

(Wh-) did + subject + verb?

Did he believe in Santa? What did SpongeBob make?
But: Who gave the clarinet? (no did when "who" is the subject)

to be

was / were

Squidward was grumpy. They were excited.
He wasn't happy. Were you surprised?

Careful! After did / didn't use the base form: did you get, didn't believe. And no did with was/were.

Підказка: правопис -ed (wrap-wrapped, try-tried, але enjoy-enjoyed); вимова /t/ /d/ /ɪd/; did + базова форма в запереченні й питанні; питання до підмета без did (Who gave you this present?); was/were без did. Типові помилки B1: «didn't went», «Did you saw?», «Who did give?».

7Reading: the night the lights went out readingteacher + student

Read the story. Find 10 irregular verbs in the Past Simple. Then answer True or False.

The Christmas the lights went out

Last December my grandma came to stay with us for the holidays. On Christmas Eve we decorated the tree, hung the stockings and wrapped the last presents. My brother and I hid a big box of chocolates for Grandma behind the sofa.

In the evening it was so cold that the water in the garden froze. Then, just before dinner, all the lights went out! Dad lit some candles, and Grandma told us stories about her childhood. She said that when she was little, she didn't get many presents, but she always found an orange in her stocking.

At midnight the clock struck twelve, and the lights suddenly came back on. We opened the presents. My brother tore the paper so fast that he almost broke his new game! But the best moment was Grandma's face when she found the chocolates. "This was the best Christmas ever," she said. And she was right.

True or False?

1Grandma stayed with the family for the holidays.
2The children hid the chocolates under the bed.
3The water in the garden froze.
4Grandma got a lot of presents when she was little.
5The lights came back at midnight.
6The brother broke his new game.
Did the lights ever go out at your home? What did you do?
What is the best Christmas or New Year you remember?

Відповіді: 1 True · 2 False · 3 True · 4 False · 5 True · 6 False. Неправильні дієслова: came, hung, hid, froze, went, lit, told, said, didn't get, found, struck, tore, broke, was.
