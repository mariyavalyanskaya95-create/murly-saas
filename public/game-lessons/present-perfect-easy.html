2Present Perfect: easy rules grammarteacher + student

Present Perfect = have / has + V3. We use it for the past that is important NOW. When it happened is not important.

1. Life experience

ever / never

I have been to London. (when? not important)
I have never eaten sushi.

2. Result now

something happened, it matters now

I have lost my keys. (I can't open the door now!)

3. Just / already / yet

recent news

I've just eaten. She's already done it. We haven't finished yet.

FormulaExample
+I / you / we / they have + V3
he / she / it has + V3		I've seen it. She's visited Paris.
−haven't / hasn't + V3I haven't seen it. He hasn't finished.
?Have / Has + subject + V3?Have you seen it? Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
V3: regular verbs = like Past Simple (visited, played). Irregular verbs = the 3rd form: go-went-gone, see-saw-seen, eat-ate-eaten.
been or gone?
  • She has been to Rome = she went and came back.
  • She has gone to Rome = she is there now.

Підказка: Present Perfect = минуле, важливе зараз. Час не називаємо. Три випадки: досвід (ever/never), результат зараз, just/already/yet. Слова-підказки: ever (колись), never (ніколи), just (щойно), already (вже), yet (ще не / вже у питанні). Типові помилки: «She have», «I have went», «I have seen him yesterday».

3Present Perfect or Past Simple? grammarteacher + student

Easy rule: exact time in the past (yesterday, last year, ago) = Past Simple. No time, experience or result = Present Perfect.

Present Perfect (no time)Past Simple (exact time)
I have been to Spain.I went to Spain last summer.
Have you ever eaten sushi?Did you eat sushi yesterday?
I have lost my phone! (it's gone now)I lost my phone two days ago.
ever, never, just, already, yetyesterday, last week, ago, in 2020, when
A typical dialogue
  • A: Have you ever been to the sea?
  • B: Yes, I have. I went to Odesa two years ago. It was amazing!

Пояснення: питаємо в Present Perfect («Have you ever...?»), деталі розповідаємо в Past Simple (when, where, how).

Ella's bucket list

Hi! I'm Ella and I'm 12. I have a "bucket list": a list of things I want to do in my life. I have already done some of them!

I have swum with dolphins. That happened in Egypt two years ago, and it was the best day of my life. I have also ridden a horse, and I have climbed a real mountain with my dad. I have seen the Eiffel Tower, but I haven't been to the top yet.

But there are many things I have never done. I have never flown in a helicopter. I have never tried Japanese food, and I have never seen snow! My friend Mia has seen snow many times. She lives in Finland.

This week I have just added a new thing to my list: learn to play the guitar. Have you got a bucket list? What have you done?

10Homework homeworkteacher + student

Памятка: Present Perfect простыми словами
  • Как строится: have / has + 3-я форма глагола (V3). I, you, we, they → have; he, she, it → has. Пример: I have seen. She has seen.
  • Когда нужен: говорим о том, что было в прошлом, но важно СЕЙЧАС, а точное время не называем: опыт в жизни (I have been to London), результат (I have lost my keys, ключей нет сейчас), новости (I have just eaten).
  • Отрицание: haven't / hasn't + V3: I haven't finished yet. Вопрос: Have / Has + кто + V3? Have you ever seen snow? Ответ: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
  • Слова-маркеры: ever (когда-нибудь), never (никогда), just (только что), already (уже), yet (ещё не / уже в вопросе), before (раньше), recently (недавно), so far (пока что), today, this week (период ещё идёт), for (в течение), since (с какого-то момента).
  • Главное отличие от Past Simple: есть точное время (yesterday, last year, ago, in 2023) → Past Simple: I went to Spain last summer. Времени нет → Present Perfect: I have been to Spain.
  • Частые ошибки: «She have» → She has. «I have went» → I have gone / been. «I have seen him yesterday» → I saw him yesterday.
