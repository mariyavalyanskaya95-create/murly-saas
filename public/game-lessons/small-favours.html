1. Your elderly neighbour is carrying four heavy shopping bags up the stairs.

6Past Perfect: the rewind button grammarteacher + student

We tell a story in the Past Simple. When we want to say that something happened even earlier, we use the Past Perfect: had + V3.

Form

had + V3

I had helped. She'd left.
I hadn't seen it.
Had you met him before?

Two actions

earlier = Past Perfect

When I got to the station, the train had already left.
(1. the train left, 2. I arrived)

Signal words

already, just, before, by the time, never ... before

By the time we arrived, they had eaten everything!

Rewind button: the whole story is in the past. Had + V3 presses rewind and goes one more step back.

Choose the correct form

1When we arrived, the film ___ already.
2She was tired because she ___ all day.
3I ___ such a kind person before.
4He thanked the boy who ___ him.
5By the time the doctor came, the man ___ better.

Put the verbs into the Past Perfect

1The old man smiled. Somebody ___ (carry) his bags.
2I couldn't pay because I ___ (lose) my wallet.
3When I got home, my sister ___ (cook) dinner.
4She felt better after her friend ___ (cheer) her up.
5Nobody ___ (do) anything like that for me before.

Асоціація: Past Perfect це кнопка «перемотати назад». Покажіть жестом: долоня вперед = Past Simple, долоня ще далі назад за плече = Past Perfect. Типові помилки: «had went» (треба had gone), Past Perfect там, де дії йдуть по черзі (I woke up, had a shower, left: тут Past Simple).

7Reading: the coffee that came back readingteacher + student

Photo: Unsplash

The coffee that came back

Last winter Mia was having a terrible morning. She had overslept, she had spilt tea on her favourite jumper, and when she got to the café near her office in Manchester, she realised she had left her wallet at home.

“It's OK,” said the man behind her in the queue. “I'll get this one.” Before Mia could say anything, he had already paid for her coffee and walked out.

Mia felt embarrassed, but also warm inside. Nobody had done anything like that for her before. So the next Friday she paid for the coffee of a student who was counting coins at the till. The student told the barista, and the barista started a “coffee wall”: people could pay for an extra coffee and leave a note for someone who needed it.

A month later Mia came back to the café. The wall was full of notes. One of them said: “A stranger had paid for my coffee on the worst day of my year. Today I'm paying for yours.” Mia smiled. She recognised the handwriting. It was from the man who had started it all.

True or False?

1Mia's morning started well.
2Mia had forgotten her wallet.
3The man asked Mia to pay him back.
4Mia paid for a student's coffee the next Friday.
5The barista started the coffee wall.
6The last note was from Mia's friend.
Discuss
  • What had happened to Mia before she got to the café?
  • Why did Mia feel warm inside?
  • Would you like a coffee wall in your town? What would you write on your note?
  • Have you ever paid it forward? What happened?

Нотатка: читають по абзацу по черзі. Виділені слова це Past Perfect: попросіть пояснити, що сталося раніше в кожному реченні.

9Role-play speakingteacher + student

Student A has a problem. Student B offers help. Use 2 phrases from the last task and 1 idiom. Then swap.

1. A: You are moving house on Saturday and nobody can help. B: You are free on Saturday.
2. A: You have a speaking exam tomorrow and you are very nervous. B: You passed the same exam last year.
3. A: You are going on holiday and you have a cat. B: You live next door.
4. A: Your phone is dead and you are lost in London. B: You know the city well.
