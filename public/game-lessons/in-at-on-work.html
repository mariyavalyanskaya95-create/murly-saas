Murphy Blue · Unit 123 · B1

In, at, on + work talk

Prepositions of position · a reading about working anywhere · a discussion about work · 60 min

In this lesson you will:

  • Talk about where and how you work.
  • Learn when to use in, at and on for position.
  • Practise with 20 quick sentences.
  • Translate 16 sentences and a short text from Russian into English, with no hints.
  • Read a short story about a person who works "anywhere" and answer questions.
  • Discuss work: office or home, a perfect working day, your dream job.
  • Describe your workplace using in / at / on.

2In, at or on? grammarteacher + student

All three can be "в / на" in Ukrainian. Think: is the thing around you, is it a point, or is it a surface or a line?

IN

inside, surrounded: walls, borders, water
in a roomin an officein a carin the gardenin a queuein Kyivin the seain a photoin a newspaper
  • My laptop is in my bag.
  • She lives in a flat in the city centre.

AT

a point: where something happens or where you stop
at the doorat the bus stopat receptionat the traffic lightsat my deskat the top / bottomat the end of the streetat a meeting
  • Wait for me at the entrance.
  • Turn left at the traffic lights.

ON

on a surface or a line: walls, floors, roads, pages
on the tableon the wallon the flooron the ceilingon the 3rd flooron the left / righton a mapon page 12on a list
  • There's a clock on the wall.
  • Our office is on the second floor.

Be careful with these

in the corner of a room (inside)
at / on the corner of a street (outside)
in the front / back of a car
at the front / back of a building, a queue, a cinema
in a car, in a taxi
on a bus, on a train, on a plane, on a bike
in a picture / photo, in a book
on a page, on a screen, on a website
Tip: if you can stand inside it, use in. If it's a place on a map where you stop or meet, use at. If it's a surface or a line, use on.

Підказка правила для пояснення: IN = всередині, з усіх боків щось оточує (кімната, машина, місто, країна, вода, картинка). AT = точка: місце, де ми зупиняємося, зустрічаємося або де щось відбувається (зупинка, двері, рецепція, світлофор, стіл у значенні «за столом»: at my desk). ON = поверхня або лінія (стіл, стіна, підлога, поверх, сторінка, дорога, ліворуч / праворуч). Пастки: in a car, але on a bus / train / plane (у великий транспорт, де можна стояти й ходити, ставимо on); in the corner of the room, але at / on the corner of the street; at the top / bottom of the page; in a photo, але on a screen. Переклад: a queue = черга · reception = рецепція · the traffic lights = світлофор · the ceiling = стеля · the entrance = вхід · a surface = поверхня · the corner = кут / ріг.

5Reading: Working anywhere readingteacher + student

Read the story (about 250 words). First read for the main idea: where does Marta work during the week? Then click "Show prepositions" and explain each one.

Working anywhere

Marta works for a small design company, but she doesn't sit in an office every day. On Mondays and Tuesdays she works at home. Her desk is in the corner of the living room, next to the window, and there is a big plant on the floor beside it.

On Wednesdays she goes to a coworking space in the city centre. It is on the third floor of an old building on Market Street. When she arrives, she leaves her bike at the entrance and takes the lift. Inside, people sit at long wooden tables. Some of them are on video calls, others are reading something on their laptops. There is a small kitchen at the end of the corridor. Marta's favourite place is at the window, because she can watch the people in the street below.

On Thursdays the whole team meets at the client's office. It is a modern building near the airport, so Marta usually goes by train. She likes working on the train: she answers emails and plans her day. On Fridays the team often has lunch at a small café on the corner.

"I don't need the same desk every day," Marta says. "I need good Wi-Fi, a quiet place and coffee. With those three things, I can work anywhere: at home, in a café or on a train."

True or false? Correct the false sentences.

1. Marta works in an office every day.
2. Her desk at home is in the corner of the living room.
3. The coworking space is on the first floor.
4. People in the coworking space sit at long tables.
5. Her favourite place is at the window.
6. She goes to the client's office by bus.
7. She doesn't like working on the train.
8. On Fridays the team has lunch at a café.
Would you like Marta's week? Why / why not?
Which of her places would be the best for you: at home, in a coworking space, on a train?
