3Reading readingteacher + student

Mia’s screen time challenge

Mia is fifteen and she lives in London. She uses TikTok and Instagram every day. She usually posts photos at the weekend, but she never shares her address or her school online. Her profile is private, and she only accepts people she knows.

Last month Mia looked at her screen time and got a shock: it was about three hours a day! She often scrolled TikTok before bed and then couldn’t sleep. She also got more than a hundred notifications every day.

So Mia started a challenge. Now she turns off notifications after 9 p.m. and she always leaves her phone in the kitchen at night. She sometimes still watches videos, but only for 30 minutes after homework. Her screen time is now one and a half hours a day. "I have more time for drawing and for my friends," Mia says. "And I sleep much better!"

Відповіді: after 9 p.m. · one and a half hours

Перший раз учень читає швидко (2 хвилини) і відповідає на питання про головну ідею. Потім читає ще раз для деталей. Після перевірки попроси знайти в тексті всі прислівники частоти (usually, never, often, always, sometimes) і підкреслити їх: це місток до граматики. 15 хвилин.

5Listening and speaking listeningteacher + student

Do you want to try the challenge?

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

Max: Hey Sophie, why didn’t you answer my message yesterday?

Sophie: Sorry! I’m trying a new challenge. I never use my phone after nine.

Max: Seriously? I always scroll before bed. It helps me relax.

Sophie: I usually did that too, but then I couldn’t sleep. Now I read a book instead.

Max: And what about your followers? Don’t they miss your posts?

Sophie: I still post, but only at the weekend. My real friends can call me.

Max: Hmm, maybe I can try it. But only for one week!

Sophie: Deal! Let’s compare our screen time next Friday.

Listen. True or False?

1Sophie doesn’t use her phone after nine.
2Max never scrolls before bed.
3Sophie stopped posting completely.
4Max agrees to try the challenge for a week.

Відповіді: True · False · False · True

Is three hours of screen time a lot? Why?
What should you never share online?
Role-play: you are Max and Sophie one week later. Did the challenge work?
What is one good thing and one bad thing about social media?

Аудіо прослухати двічі: спершу без завдання (хто про що говорить), потім із True/False. Далі рольова гра: учень і вчитель - Max і Sophie через тиждень. Учень має вжити мінімум 2 прислівники частоти і 2 нові слова. Виправляй м’яко, повторюючи фразу правильно. 14 хвилин.

Секції можна дати наприкінці уроку або як розминку на наступному. Нове тут hardly ever (майже ніколи): воно стоїть там само, де never. Нагадай про to be: She is usually online. У першій секції blocked у минулому часі, бо в реченні є sent. У перекладі «гортаю стрічку» зараховуються scroll, scroll my feed і scroll through my feed. Після гри попроси учня сказати про себе по одному реченню з кожним прислівником.
