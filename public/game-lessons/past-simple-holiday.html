3Past Simple: quick review grammarteacher + student

Read the rules. Say three sentences about yesterday: one positive, one negative, one question.

Regular verbs

verb + -ed

travel → travelled · pack → packed
stop → stopped · study → studied · live → lived

Irregular verbs

learn them!

go → went · see → saw · lose → lost · find → found · take → took · get → got · eat → ate · buy → bought

Negative

didn't + verb

We didn't go to the beach. (NOT "didn't went")

Questions

Did ... + verb?

Did you like it? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Where did you go?

to be

was / were

I / he / she / it was · we / you / they were
It wasn't fun. Were you tired?

Careful! After didn't and did the verb has NO -ed and no past form: didn't see, Did you go?

Підказка для пояснення: правильні дієслова + -ed; правопис: подвоєння приголосної після короткого наголошеного голосного (stop-stopped, plan-planned), y після приголосної → ied (study-studied, але play-played), -e → +d (live-lived). Заперечення й питання з did/didn't + базова форма. Was/were для to be (без did!). Типові помилки: «didn't went», «Did you went?», «I was go».

6Reading: Mia's holiday readingteacher + student

Photo: Osman Rana / Unsplash

Read the story. Find 10 verbs in the Past Simple. Which are regular? Which are irregular? Then answer True or False.

Mia's terrible (and amazing) holiday

Last August Mia and her family went on holiday to the seaside. They planned everything, but nothing went right.

First, they missed their train because Mia's little brother couldn't find his shoes. They took the next train and arrived three hours late. When they got to the hotel, there was a problem: the hotel didn't have their booking! The receptionist was very kind, and she found them a room, but it was tiny and the window didn't open.

The next day it rained. And the day after that. And the day after that! Mia's mum bought umbrellas, and they visited a museum, an aquarium and a chocolate factory. Mia ate so much chocolate that she felt sick.

On the last day the sun finally came out. They spent the whole day on the beach. Mia swam in the sea, built a sandcastle with her brother and took 200 photos. In the evening they watched the sunset and ate pizza.

"It was the worst holiday ever," Mia's dad said on the train home. "No," said Mia. "It was the best!"

True or False?

1Mia's family went to the mountains.
2They missed the train because of the brother's shoes.
3The hotel had their booking.
4Their room was very big.
5It rained for several days.
6They visited a chocolate factory.
7Mia didn't swim on the last day.
8Mia thought it was the best holiday.
What went wrong? Name three things.
Why did Mia think it was the best holiday?
Did you ever have a holiday like this?

Відповіді: 1 False · 2 True · 3 False · 4 False · 5 True · 6 True · 7 False · 8 True. Неправильні дієслова в тексті: went, couldn't, took, got, was, found, bought, ate, felt, came, spent, swam, built, said. Правильні: planned, missed, arrived, rained, visited, watched.

Переклад: the seaside = морське узбережжя · a booking = бронювання · a receptionist = адміністратор · tiny = крихітний · an umbrella = парасолька · an aquarium = акваріум · to feel sick = нудити · the sunset = захід сонця · a sandcastle = замок з піску.

7Listening: Tom's trip to London listeningteacher + student

Photo: Marco López / Unsplash

Read the questions. Listen to Tom and choose the answers.

Hello! My name is Tom. Last spring I went to London with my class. We travelled by plane. It was my first flight, and I was a bit scared. On the first day we visited the Tower of London and saw the crown jewels. They were amazing! In the afternoon we got lost in a big park, but a friendly police officer helped us. On the second day it rained, so we went to the Natural History Museum. I loved the dinosaurs. I bought a small dinosaur for my little sister. We didn't have time to see Big Ben, but I want to go back next year.
1Where did Tom go?
2How did they travel?
3What did they see at the Tower of London?
4Who helped them in the park?
5What did Tom buy for his sister?

Відповіді: 1 London · 2 by plane · 3 the crown jewels · 4 a police officer · 5 a small dinosaur. Потім спитайте: «What didn't they see?» (Big Ben).
