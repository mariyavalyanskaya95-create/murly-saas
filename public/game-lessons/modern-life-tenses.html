4Four tenses, one story grammarteacher + student

Read the table. Then explain to the teacher: what is the difference between "I play" and "I am playing"? Between "I played" and "I was playing"?

TenseUse it forExampleSignal words
Present Simple
I check / he checks		habits, facts, routinesI check my phone 100 times a day.always, usually, every day, never
Present Continuous
I am checking		now, around now, temporary situations, changesI'm trying to use my phone less this month.now, at the moment, these days, this week
Past Simple
I checked / went		finished actions in the past, a story's main eventsI turned off my phone and went outside.yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020, then
Past Continuous
I was checking		background, an action in progress in the pastI was scrolling when my mum came in.while, when, at 9 pm yesterday, all evening

Long + short

was/were + -ing ... when + past simple

I was doing my homework when my phone buzzed.

Two long actions

while + past continuous

While I was studying, my friends were playing online.

One after another

past simple, past simple

I woke up, checked my phone and got angry.

No -ing!

state verbs

know, want, need, like, believe, understand: I know (not "I'm knowing").

Careful! "These days I am spending too much time online" = a temporary situation now. "I spend 3 hours online every day" = a habit.

Підказка для пояснення: PS = звичка, факт; PC = зараз або тимчасово («these days»); PaS = завершена дія, головні події історії; PaC = фон, дія в процесі в минулому. Формула історії: Past Continuous (фон) + when + Past Simple (подія). While + Past Continuous. Дієслова стану (know, want, need, like) не мають -ing. Типові помилки: «I was knowing», «When I was come», «I am usually play».

6Reading: The day my phone died readingteacher + student

Photo: Kelly Sikkema / Unsplash

Read the story. Find one example of each tense: Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, Past Continuous. Then answer True or False.

The day my phone died

I usually check my phone the moment I wake up. I read my messages, I look at the news and I scroll through TikTok before I even get out of bed. So last Tuesday was a shock.

I was walking to the bus stop and listening to music when my phone suddenly switched off. The battery was dead, and I had forgotten my charger at home. At first I felt anxious. What if somebody was trying to call me? What if my friends were chatting about something important?

On the bus, everybody was looking at their screens. Nobody was talking. For the first time, I looked out of the window. I noticed a small café that I had never seen before, and an old man who was feeding birds in the park.

At school, while my classmates were checking their notifications during the break, I talked to a new student. His name is Leo and he is from Spain. We laughed a lot, and now we are planning to start a band together!

When I got home and charged my phone, I had 47 notifications. Most of them were not important at all. These days I am trying to leave my phone in my bag more often. I still love it, but I don't want it to control my life.

True or False?

1The writer usually checks the phone right after waking up.
2The phone died because it fell in water.
3At first, the writer felt anxious without the phone.
4On the bus, people were talking to each other.
5The writer met a new student during the break.
6Leo is from Portugal.
7Most of the 47 notifications were important.
8Now the writer is trying to use the phone less.
What did the writer notice for the first time? Why?
Has something like this ever happened to you? What did you do?
Could you live without your phone for one day? One week?

Відповіді: 1 True · 2 False · 3 True · 4 False · 5 True · 6 False · 7 False · 8 True. Приклади часів: PS «I usually check», PC «we are planning / I am trying», PaS «my phone suddenly switched off», PaC «I was walking ... when». Також є Past Perfect («I had forgotten», «had never seen»): можна згадати як бонус.

Переклад: the moment I wake up = як тільки прокидаюся · battery was dead = батарея сіла · a charger = зарядка · to notice = помітити · to feed birds = годувати птахів · to start a band = створити гурт · to control = контролювати.
