3НМТ Reading 1: Sleep and teenagers readingteacher + student

Прочитай абзац і сформулюй його головну думку своїми словами. Лише потім шукай заголовок. Пастка: заголовок з тим самим словом, що й у тексті, часто неправильний. Головне не слова, а зміст. На НМТ заголовків більше, ніж абзаців, тож деякі залишаться зайвими.

Дайте 7 хвилин на текст і заголовки. Тут уже є два зайві заголовки: «Teenagers who sleep too much» і «Why adults need less sleep». Після перевірки обговоріть, чому вони не підходять жодному абзацу: перший суперечить тексту, другого в тексті немає взагалі.

A. Many parents complain that their teenage children stay up late and cannot get out of bed in the morning. Scientists say this is not just laziness. During adolescence, the body starts producing the sleep hormone later in the evening, so teenagers naturally feel sleepy later.

B. Unfortunately, school still starts early. As a result, many students sleep only six or seven hours, while experts recommend eight to ten. Tired students find it harder to concentrate, remember new information and control their emotions.

C. Some schools have tried a later start. In several studies, when lessons began an hour later, students slept longer, were late less often and even got better grades.

D. Phones make the problem worse. The bright screen tells the brain that it is still daytime, and endless videos and messages keep us awake. Doctors advise putting the phone away at least an hour before going to bed.

Choose the best heading for each paragraph. Two headings are extra.

1Paragraph A: ___
2Paragraph B: ___
3Paragraph C: ___
4Paragraph D: ___

Відповіді: It’s biology, not laziness · Not enough rest for learning · An experiment that worked · A screen that steals your sleep

4НМТ Reading 2: Board games are back readingteacher + student

Заголовки НМТ майже завжди перефразовують текст. «Advice for beginners» у тексті може звучати як «If you want to start…». Підкресли в абзаці фразу, яка доводить твій вибір.

Цей текст учень виконує на час: 6 хвилин. Зайві заголовки тут: «Games that only adults can play» і «How to win every time». Другий навмисно схожий на абзац E: в абзаці сказано якраз протилежне (don’t worry about winning). Друга вправа тренує пошук перефразувань.

A. Ten years ago, many people thought board games would soon disappear. Today, cafés where you can play board games are opening in cities all over the world, and sales of games have grown every year.

B. Why? Many young people say they are tired of screens. After a day of online lessons and messages, sitting around a table with real cards and dice feels refreshing. “It’s the only time my friends and I put our phones away,” says Oliver, 16.

C. Board games are not only fun. Strategy games teach you to plan ahead, and cooperative games, where all the players work together against the game, build teamwork. Some teachers now use them in maths and language lessons.

D. Not everyone is a fan, though. Some games take hours to learn, and the rule books can be thicker than a school textbook. Good games can also be expensive, which is why many players borrow them from libraries or game clubs.

E. If you want to start, choose a short, simple game for three or four players. Invite friends who don’t usually play, and don’t worry about winning. The best part is the conversation, not the score.

Choose the best heading for each paragraph. Two headings are extra.

Відповіді: An old hobby makes a comeback · A break from the digital world · More than just entertainment · A few disadvantages · Advice for beginners

Extra practice можна виконати, якщо лишився час, або на початку наступного уроку. У заголовках учень спершу формулює головну думку міні-абзацу своїми словами, потім обирає варіант.
