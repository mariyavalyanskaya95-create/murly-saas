2Grammar map ruleexplain + check

Past Simple = a finished action in the past. One past form only in (+). In (-) and (?) the past is in did.

+ Positive

I / He / They + V2
  • I played football. (regular: +ed)
  • She went home. (irregular: V2)
  • We were tired. He was late.

- Negative

didn't + V1
  • I didn't go to school.
  • He didn't see me.
  • BUT: I wasn't / they weren't (no did with be!)

? Question

Did + subject + V1?
  • Did you go out? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  • What did you do?
  • BUT: Were you at home? Was it fun?
TRAP! did already shows the past. The verb goes back to V1.
I didn't went go. · Did you saw see it? · She didn't made make it.
