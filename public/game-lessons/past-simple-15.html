2Grammar map ruleexplain + check

Past Simple = a finished action in the past. One past form only in (+). In (-) and (?) the past is in did.

+ Positive

I / He / They + V2
  • I played football. (regular: +ed)
  • She went home. (irregular: V2)
  • We were tired. He was late.

- Negative

didn't + V1
  • I didn't go to school.
  • He didn't see me.
  • BUT: I wasn't / they weren't (no did with be!)

? Question

Did + subject + V1?
  • Did you go out? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  • What did you do?
  • BUT: Were you at home? Was it fun?
TRAP! did already shows the past. The verb goes back to V1.
I didn't went go. · Did you saw see it? · She didn't made make it.

    15Reading: The day I nearly missed my exam readingread · find · answer

    A. Read the story aloud. B. Find the Past Simple of these verbs in the text and write them (no peeking at the cards!). C. Answer the questions in full sentences.

    The day I nearly missed my exam

    Last May I had my first big exam. The night before, I thought I was ready, so I went to bed late and watched videos until two in the morning. That was a mistake.

    In the morning my alarm didn't ring. I woke up at 8:40, and the exam started at 9:00! I got dressed in thirty seconds, took my passport and ran to the bus stop. Of course, the bus didn't come.

    Then I saw my neighbour, Mr Petrenko, in his car. I told him about the exam, and he said, "Get in!" He drove very fast, and we came to the school at 8:58. I found my classroom, sat down and took a deep breath.

    The exam was hard, but I knew most of the answers. Two weeks later I got my result: 186 points! I gave Mr Petrenko a big box of chocolates. Now I always go to bed early before an important day.
