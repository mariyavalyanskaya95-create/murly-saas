4The rule: the jumping verb grammarteacher + student

In a question, am / is / are jumps to the start, before the person. Like a cat that jumps on the table first.

Yes / No questions

Am / Is / Are + who + ...?

You are hungry. → Are you hungry?
She is at home. → Is she at home?

Short answers

Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Is he 14? Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
Are they friends? Yes, they are.

Wh- questions

Where / What / Who + am / is / are

Where are you from?
How old is your sister?
Who is that boy?

Careful: «Yes» answers are never short: Yes, I am (not «Yes, I'm»). Age is with to be: How old are you?

Інтонація: у Yes / No питаннях голос іде вгору ↗, у питаннях з where / what вниз ↘. Повторіть з учнем кілька разів.
Чому «Yes, I am» без скорочення: наголос падає на дієслово, тому кажемо повністю.
«Скільки тобі років?» англійською буквально «Наскільки ти старий?» = How old are you?

6Picture questions speakingteacher + student

Look at the photos. Ask your teacher 3 questions about each photo with Is ... ? / Are ... ? / Where ... ? The teacher answers. Then swap!

Question starters
  • Are they friends / sisters / classmates?
  • Is she happy / tired / cool?
  • Where are they? Is it a café?
  • How old is she? What is her name?

Зразки питань: Are they friends? Are they at school? Is it summer? Is she happy? Where is the café? How old is the girl?
Ваші відповіді: короткі й з фантазією: «Yes, they are. They are best friends from Year 8!» Потім поміняйтесь: ви питаєте, учень відповідає коротко.
