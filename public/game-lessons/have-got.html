Have you got a cat?

have got · has got · do you have · listening · video · 60 min

Photo: Unsplash

In this lesson you will:

  • Say what you have: I've got / I have.
  • Listen to two cat owners at the vet.
  • Learn the two teams: have got and do / does have.
  • Practise with 4 exercises and translation.
  • Draw and describe a monster, watch videos and speak.

2Listen: two cat owners at the vet listeningteacher + student

Show the dialogue

Lena: Hi! Is that your cat? He's huge!

Ethan: Yes, this is Pumpkin. He's got a cold, so we're here to see the vet.

Lena: Oh, poor thing. My cat has got a problem with her teeth.

Ethan: Have you got just one cat?

Lena: No, I've got two cats and a hamster. What about you?

Ethan: Only Pumpkin. But he's got a lot of toys. He has more toys than me!

Lena: Ha! Does he have a favourite toy?

Ethan: Yes, he does. A plastic fish. He hasn't got any interest in the expensive ones.

Lena: Typical cat. Have you got a photo of him with the fish?

Ethan: Yes, I have. Look! My sister has got a hundred photos of him. She's got a lot of free time.

Lena: I haven't got a sister, only a brother. And he hasn't got time for anything, only football.

True or False?

1Pumpkin has got a cold.
2Lena has got one cat.
3Lena has got a hamster.
4Pumpkin's favourite toy is a plastic fish.
5Ethan hasn't got a sister.
6Lena has got a brother.

Про що діалог: Лена й Ітан зустрілися у ветеринара. Кіт Ітана Пампкін застудився, у кішки Лени проблеми із зубами. У Лени дві кішки й хом'як, у Пампкіна купа іграшок, улюблена пластикова рибка. Сестра Ітана має сотню фото кота, а брат Лени цікавиться тільки футболом.
Відповіді: T, F, T, T, F, T.
Після: попросіть знайти в діалозі всі have / has got і does ... have.

4The rule: two teams grammarteacher + student

Have got and have mean the same: «у мене є / у меня есть». But they are two teams. Don't mix them!

Team «got»

have / has got

I've got a bike. She's got blue eyes.
I haven't got a phone.
Have you got a pet? Yes, I have.

Team «do»

have / has + do / does

I have a bike. She has blue eyes.
I don't have a phone.
Do you have a pet? Yes, I do.

he / she / it

has

My cat has got a long tail.
He's got = He has got (not «is»!)

Trap: «Do you have got...?» doesn't exist. Got and do are not friends!

Як пояснити: намалюйте дві колонки «команда got» і «команда do». Попросіть сказати одне речення двома способами: I've got a cat = I have a cat. Have got частіше в британській англійській і в розмові, have + do універсальне.

5Have got workout grammarteacher + student

A. has got or have got?

1My brother ___ a new laptop.
2I ___ two cousins.
3Our cat ___ green eyes.
4We ___ a big garden.
5The school ___ a pool.
6My parents ___ a car.

B. Write the right form

1I ___ (have got) two cousins.
2We ___ (not have got) a printer.
3___ you got a ruler? Yes, I have.
4Dan ___ (not have got) a bike.
5Mia ___ (not have) a phone. (team do)

C. Short answers: same team as the question!

1Have you got a pet? Yes, ___
2Does he have a bike? No, ___
3Has she got a phone? No, ___
4Do they have a garden? Yes, ___

D. Translate into English

1У мене є кіт.
2У моєї сестри довге волосся.
3У нас немає собаки.
4У тебе є брат?
5У нього немає телефону.

Відповіді A: has got · have got · has got · have got · has got · have got. B: have got · haven't got · Have · hasn't got · doesn't have. C: I have · he doesn't · she hasn't · they do.
На перевірці питайте: «Яка це команда, got чи do?» У перекладі приймаються обидві команди.

6Reading: meet Zog, the school monster readingteacher + student

Meet Zog, the school monster

Zog has got three eyes and two big noses. He hasn't got any hair, but he has got a long green tail. He has got six legs, so he is very fast in PE.

Zog doesn't have a phone, but he has a pet: a small blue cat with wings. They have got the same favourite food: socks!

1Has Zog got hair?
2How many eyes has Zog got?
3Does Zog have a phone?
4What has Zog's pet got?
5What is their favourite food?

Draw your own monster!

1 minute: draw a monster on paper or on the board. Then describe it: My monster has got ... It hasn't got ...

Переклад: У Зога три ока й два великі носи. Волосся немає, зате довгий зелений хвіст. Шість ніг, тому він дуже швидкий на фізкультурі. Телефону немає, але є улюбленець: маленька синя кішка з крилами. Улюблена їжа в обох: шкарпетки!
Ваш монстр-приклад: «My monster has got four arms and one big eye. It hasn't got a mouth, so it doesn't eat. It has got a pink umbrella.»
