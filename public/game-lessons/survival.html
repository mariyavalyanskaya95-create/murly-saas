Present Simple

When? Rules of the game, habits, things that are always true.

+I / you / we / theybuildhouses.
+he / she / itbuildshouses.
−I / you / we / theydon'tbuildat night.
−he / she / itdoesn'tbuildat night.
?Doyou / we / theybuildhouses?
?Doeshe / she / itbuildhouses?
Trap! After doesn't and does there is NO -s: She doesn't builds → She doesn't build.
he / she / it + s: mine → mines · go → goes · watch → watches · fly → flies · have → has

Present Continuous

When? Live stream: what is happening right now.

+Iambuildinga house.
+he / she / itisbuildinga house.
+you / we / theyarebuildinga house.
−sheisn'tbuildingnow.
?Areyoubuildingnow?
Trap! Never forget the helper: I building → I am building.
+ing: mine → mining (no e) · dig → digging · swim → swimming · play → playing
nowright nowat the momentLook!Listen!Hurry up!
Secret level: some verbs never go into live mode: know, like, love, want, need, have (= own), understand.
I know how to make a pickaxe. Not: I am knowing.

Тест (15-20 мин): Choose, Live stream, Sort, Fix the bugs. Фиксируйте ошибки, особенно -s в he/she/it и глаголы know/like/want. 7. Missions (15-20 мин): играете, ученик комментирует; если ошибается в форме, просто повторяете правильно. 8. Streamer mode (5 мин) в конце или вместо одной миссии.
