3Reading readingteacher + student

When a computer program produces a poem that moves its readers or a painting that wins a competition, it is tempting to call it creative. Yet many artists dismiss such work as clever imitation: the machine has simply learned patterns from millions of human examples and recombined them.

Supporters of AI art point out that human creativity also builds on what came before. No painter works in a vacuum; every writer has read other writers. If creativity means combining existing ideas in surprising ways, then, arguably, machines are already creative.

Critics, however, focus on intent. A human artist chooses to express something: grief, joy, anger, a memory. A program has no experiences to express and no reason to create other than the instruction it was given. From this perspective, the creativity belongs to the person who wrote the prompt, selected the best result and decided what it meant.

There is also the question of risk. Human artists often produce work that fails, offends or confuses, and that willingness to take risks is arguably part of what makes art meaningful. A model designed to give users what they are most likely to approve of may, almost by definition, play it safe.

The debate also raises practical concerns. Who owns a picture generated by AI? Should illustrators be paid when their work is used to train a model? And if anyone can produce a professional-looking image in seconds, what happens to the value of human skill?

Perhaps the most honest answer is that AI is blurring the line between tool and creator. A camera did not end painting; it changed it. Whether AI turns out to be a breakthrough for artists or a threat to them may depend less on the technology itself than on the rules we choose to build around it.

4Language focus practiceteacher + student

Hedging, тобто обережні формулювання, звучать переконливіше в дискусії: It could be argued that… / To some extent… / This may suggest that… / There seems to be… / I’m not entirely convinced that… Модальні may / might / could і дієслова seem / appear / tend to пом’якшують категоричні твердження.

Rewrite more cautiously. Complete the gaps.

1AI is creative. → It could be ___ that AI is creative.
2This is true. → To some ___, this is true.
3I don’t believe it. → I’m not entirely ___.
4AI will replace illustrators. → AI ___ replace some illustrators.
5Everyone agrees. → There ___ to be broad agreement.

Відповіді: argued · extent · convinced · may · seems

Choose the correct hedging phrase.

1___ that prompts are a form of authorship.
2The results are, ___, impressive.
3There ___ to be a growing demand for human-made art.
4This ___ that the market is changing.

Відповіді: It could be argued · to some extent · appears · would seem to suggest

Переклади англійською, використовуючи hedging.

2До певної міри я з цим згоден

Відповіді: It could be argued that AI is blurring the line between tool and creator · To some extent, I agree with this

Поясніть, що hedging не означає слабкість позиції, це академічна і ділова норма. Вправи 6-7 хв. Переклад може мати інші коректні варіанти: зараховуйте їх вручну. Типова помилка: «in some extent» замість to some extent.
