Personality · A2 · fun lesson

What are they like?

7 words about people (and cats, and dogs) · photo games · speaking · 60 min

In this lesson you will:

  • Learn 7 words about personality.
  • Guess the personality of funny animals from photos.
  • Match words with their meanings and guess "Who said it?".
  • Play Bowling: answer right and knock down the pins!
  • Describe your best friend, your family and famous characters.
  • Make the silliest sentence in the world.

1Three words warm-upteacher + student

Answer quickly. Don't think too long!

1. Describe yourself in three words. Go!
2. Who is your best friend? What is he or she like?
3. Are you more like a cat or a dog? Why?
Useful phrases
  • I'm ..., ... and ... .
  • My best friend is ... . He / She is very ... .
  • I'm more like a cat because I'm ... .

Нотатка: 4-5 хвилин. Питання «What is he like?» = «Який він (за характером)?», а не «Що він любить?». Це головна пастка уроку: What is he like? = характер, What does he like? = що любить, What does he look like? = зовнішність.

Useful phrases
  • He / She is very ... . For example, ...
  • He / She is a bit ... , but he / she is also ... .
  • He / She isn't ... at all!
  • I think he / she is ... because he / she always / never ... .

Would you rather...?

Would you rather have a friend who is crazy or boring?
Would you rather be brave or a good listener?
Would you rather go on holiday with an adventurous friend or a boring friend?
Would you rather have an arrogant best friend or no best friend?

Нотатка: «a bit» вживаємо з негативними словами (a bit boring, a bit arrogant), «very» з будь-якими. «Would you rather ... or ...?» = «Ти б краще... чи...?»; відповідь: «I'd rather ...».
