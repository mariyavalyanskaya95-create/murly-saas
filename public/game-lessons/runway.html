The adjective train grammar

Adjectives stand in a line before the noun, like wagons of a train. The order never changes!

opinioncute, stylishsizelong, tinyagenew, oldcolourred, goldpatternstripedmaterialsilk, denimNOUNdress
agorgeouslongpurplesilkdress

Trap! a red big hat → a big red hat. Size comes before colour.

Comparing two

short words+er than

long → longer than · big → bigger · pretty → prettier

long wordsmore ... than

more elegant than · more colourful than

The best of all

theshort+est

the longest · the biggest · the prettiest

the mostlong words

the most elegant · the most beautiful

Rebels: good → better → the best · bad → worse → the worst
