2Lead-in practiceteacher + student

Where are you?!

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

A: Hey, Chloe! Where are you? Are you coming?

B: Hi, Dan! Yes, I’m coming. I’m on the bus.

A: The bus? Why are you taking the bus? The park is near your house!

B: I’m not at home. I’m at my grandma’s. Wait, what are you doing?

A: I’m waiting for you! And Mia is here too.

B: What is she doing?

A: She’s eating ice cream. Your ice cream, I think.

B: What?! Is she joking?

A: No, she isn’t. She’s laughing. Hey, is your cat travelling with you? I can hear a cat.

B: Yes, he is. Grandma is giving him back to me. He’s meowing in his bag.

3Rule practiceteacher + student

Питання в Present Continuous будуємо так само, як питання з to be: am / is / are стрибає на початок, а -ing залишається на місці.

You are reading. → Are you reading? = Ти читаєш?
He is sleeping. → Is he sleeping? = Він спить?

Короткі відповіді (без -ing!):
Are you working? → Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Is she crying? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.

Питальне слово + am / is / are + хто? + -ing?
What are you doing? · Where is she going? · Why are you laughing? · Who are you talking to?

Лайфхак: What are you doing? це «чарівна фраза». Запам’ятай її цілком, як пароль від Wi-Fi.

Порівняй з питаннями to be: схема та сама, тільки додається -ing. Окремо покажи «Who are you talking to?»: прийменник у кінці.

5Video: Pause and ask videoteacher + student

Watch and pause. Ask 3 questions about the scene: What is he doing? Is he…? Why is he…?

Watch and answer with short answers: Is Mr Bean swimming? Is he jumping? Are the people watching him?

Перед уроком переглянь відео і обери 3-4 паузи (щоразу, коли Mr Bean починає щось нове, орієнтовно кожні 40-60 секунд). На паузах ролі міняються: спочатку питаєш ти (What is he doing? Is he smiling?), учень відповідає коротко (Yes, he is. / No, he isn’t. He’s…). Потім учень ставить питання тобі. Очікувані питання: What is he holding? Why is he laughing? Is the teacher looking at him? Are the people watching? Для Just Dive Bean: Is he standing on the diving board? Is he scared? Is he jumping? Якщо часу мало, візьми одне відео.

6Розбір помилок practiceteacher + student

Типові помилки:

What you are doing? → What are you doing? (am / is / are перед підметом)
You are doing what? → What are you doing? (питальне слово на початок)
Do you reading? → Are you reading? (do тут не потрібне, тільки am / is / are)
Yes, I’m. → Yes, I am. (у короткій відповіді «так» не скорочуємо)
To who are you talking? → Who are you talking to? (прийменник у кінці)

7Speaking speakingteacher + student

Role play: call a friend. Ask: Where are you? What are you doing? Who are you with?
Guess: What is your mum or dad doing right now?
Look at a photo on your phone. Ask me 3 questions about it, I’ll answer.
Are you learning anything new these days? What?
Are you reading or watching anything interesting this week?
What game or series are your friends talking about these days?
Is your cat planning something evil right now? What is it doing?
Imagine I’m calling you from the moon. Ask me 3 questions.
What is your favourite singer doing right now, do you think?
Why are you smiling? (Answer with a funny reason!)

Головне тут рольова гра «Phone call». Учень «дзвонить» тобі, ти в смішній ситуації (ти в зоопарку, і мавпа краде твій бутерброд). Учень мусить з’ясувати питаннями, де ти, що робиш і чому смієшся. Потім міняєтеся. Нагадай, що Present Continuous також для дій «у ці дні» (I’m reading a great book this week).
