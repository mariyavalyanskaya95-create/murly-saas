4Present Simple: the little thieves grammarteacher + student

Present Simple = habits, routines, facts, timetables: what you usually do.

Good guys
Iyouwethey
We play games.
We don't play games.
Do we play games?
Little thieves
hesheit
s
He plays games.
He doesn't play games.
Does he play games?
The story: he, she and it are little thieves. They wanted to steal dollars, but they only stole the letter s. Now they always put it on the verb: she works, he plays, it rains. In negatives and questions the s hides in does, so the verb has no s: she doesn't work, does he play?

Spelling

-s · -es · -ies

play → plays
watch, go, do, wash → watches, goes, does
study, fly → studies, flies
have → has

Signal words: when do we use Present Simple?

alwaysвсегдаI always drink tea in the morning.
usuallyобычноShe usually gets up at 7.
oftenчастоWe often hang out after school.
sometimesиногдаI sometimes cook dinner.
rarelyредкоHe rarely watches TV.
neverникогдаShe never eats meat.
every day / every weekкаждый день / каждую неделюI walk my dog every day.
once / twice / three times a weekраз / два / три раза в неделюShe goes to the gym three times a week.
on Mondays / at weekendsпо понедельникам / по выходнымWe play football at weekends.
in the morning / in the eveningутром / вечеромI read in the evening.
Where? always, usually, often, sometimes, never go before the verb (I often read) but after to be (I am often late).

Как объяснить: расскажите историю про воришек: «he, she, it хотели украсть доллары, но украли только букву S, и теперь прицепляют её к глаголу». В отрицании и вопросе буква прячется в does, поэтому глагол без s: She doesn't like, Does she like?
Маркеры: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, once a week, on Mondays, at weekends. Попросите поставить их по «шкале»: от always (100%) до never (0%).
Проверка: «My mum ___ (work) every day» → works. «My mum doesn't ___ (work)» → work.

6Reading: a day in the life of Lily readingteacher + student

Photo: Unsplash

A day in the life of Lily

Lily is 14 and she lives in Bath, a small city in England. She is a bit shy, but she is very funny with her friends.

On weekdays she wakes up at 7, but she never gets up straight away. She usually scrolls through her phone for ten minutes. Then she gets dressed, has toast with jam and catches the bus at 8.

Lily is good at art, and she is really into drawing. She draws cartoons of her teachers, but she never shows them! She can't stand PE, especially in winter.

After school she takes care of her dog, Biscuit. Twice a week she has guitar lessons. At weekends she hangs out with her best friend Ella. They often go to a café and chill out. Lily goes to bed at 10, but she doesn't fall asleep before 11.

1Lily lives in a big city.
2She gets up straight away.
3She is into drawing.
4She loves PE.
5She has guitar lessons twice a week.
6She falls asleep at 10.
What does Lily do in the morning?
What is she good at?
What can't she stand?
What does she do at weekends?
Are you like Lily? What is the same, what is different?

Ответы T/F: F, F, T, F, T, F.
Перевод:
1. Лили 14 лет, она живёт в Бате, маленьком городе в Англии. Она немного стеснительная, но очень смешная с друзьями.

2. По будням она просыпается в 7, но никогда не встаёт сразу. Обычно она десять минут листает телефон. Потом одевается, ест тост с джемом и успевает на автобус в 8.

3. Лили хорошо рисует и очень увлекается рисованием. Она рисует карикатуры на учителей, но никогда их не показывает! Она терпеть не может физкультуру, особенно зимой.

4. После школы она заботится о своей собаке Бисквит. Два раза в неделю у неё уроки гитары. На выходных она гуляет с лучшей подругой Эллой. Они часто ходят в кафе и отдыхают. Лили ложится спать в 10, но не засыпает раньше 11.
