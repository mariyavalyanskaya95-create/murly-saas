This is my family

Family words · have got / has got · listening · speaking · 60 min

Photo: Unsplash

In this lesson you will:

  • Learn 12 family words.
  • Listen to Max talking about his family.
  • Say what you have got: I have got, she has got.
  • Practise with exercises and translation.
  • Describe a picture and present your family.

1Hello! Is your family big? warm-upteacher + student

Photo: Unsplash

Is your family big or small?

Нотатка: попросіть дитину заздалегідь підготувати фото сім'ї або намалювати її. Якщо фото немає, намалюйте разом прості фігурки. Тут не виправляйте, просто розговоріть.

3Listen: Max shows a photo listeningteacher + student

Show the dialogue

Anna: Max, is this your family?

Max: Yes, it is! This is my mum and this is my dad.

Anna: Who is this girl?

Max: This is my sister. Her name is Lily. She is ten.

Anna: She has got long hair.

Max: Yes, she has. And this is my grandma and grandpa. They live with us.

Anna: Have you got a brother?

Max: No, I haven't. But I have got a cousin. His name is Tom.

Anna: Has Tom got a pet?

Max: Yes, he has. He has got a funny dog called Biscuit.

Anna: I love dogs! Have you got a pet, Max?

Max: No, I haven't. But I want a cat!

True or False?

1Lily is Max's sister.
2Lily is eight.
3Max's grandma and grandpa live with him.
4Max has got a brother.
5Biscuit is a cat.

Choose

1Lily has got ___ hair.
2Max's cousin is called ___.
3Max wants a ___.

Перед слуханням: «Max shows his family photo. Who can you see?» Перше слухання загальне, друге для деталей. Під час слухання дитина піднімає руку, коли чує слово з карток.
Відповіді: T, F, T, F, F · long · Tom · cat.

4The rule: have got, has got grammarteacher + student

I / you / we / they

have got

I have got a sister.
We have got a cat.

he / she / it

has got

She has got a cat.
Grandpa has got a red car.

Questions

Have / Has ... got?

Have you got a brother?
Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

Гра «Family cards»: картки з членами сім'ї й тваринами. Дитина тягне картку й каже: I have got a... Потім про вас: You have got... І про іграшку: She has got...
Підказка для дитини: «Один хтось (він, вона, бабуся, Макс) = has. Я, ти, ми, вони = have.»

5Family workout grammarteacher + student

A. have got or has got?

1I ___ a big family.
2Lily ___ long hair.
3We ___ a cat.
4My grandpa ___ a red car.
5My aunt and uncle ___ a car.

B. Write have or has

1___ you got a sister?
2___ Max got a brother?
3My cousin ___ got a dog.
4They ___ got a baby.

C. Short answers

1Has your dad got a car? Yes, he ___.
2Have you got a pet? No, I ___.
3Has Max got a brother? No, he ___.

D. Translate into English

1Це мій тато.
2У мене є сестра.
3У нього є собака.
4У тебе є брат?
5У мене немає домашнього улюбленця.

Відповіді A: have got · has got · have got · has got · have got. B: Have · Has · has · have. C: has · haven't · hasn't.
Порада: для дітей перекладайте усно, а вписує дитина тільки одне-два речення.

6Describe the picture speakingteacher + student

Look at the photo. Who is it? Make up a family! This is ... She has got ... They have got ...

Зразок до фото 1: «This is Grandma Rose and this is Grandma Lily. They are sisters. This is Emma. She is their granddaughter. Emma has got long hair. They have got tea and cakes!»
Навідні питання: Who is she? Is she a grandma? Has she got long hair? Have they got a cat?

7Speaking: my family speakingteacher + student

Who is in your family?
Have you got a brother or a sister?
Have you got a grandma? Where does she live?
Have you got a pet? What is its name?
Help
  • This is my ... His / Her name is ...
  • He / She has got ...
  • I have got ... / I haven't got ...

Нотатка: дитина показує фото чи малюнок і презентує сім'ю. Ви ставите уточнювальні питання. Потім міняєтесь: ви описуєте вигадану смішну сім'ю (My grandma has got a pink motorbike!), дитина питає.

8Speed game: have got or has got? gameteacher + student

Нотатка: «have got» повторюється, тому будь-яка пара з have got правильна для I / We / They. Засічіть 30 секунд.

9Homework: my family tree homeworkteacher + student

Max's text

Hello! I'm Max. I'm eight. I have got a big family. I have got one sister. Her name is Lily. She has got long hair. My grandma and grandpa live with us. My cousin Tom has got a funny dog called Biscuit.

Write about your family

4-5 sentences, like Max.

Draw a family tree

Draw your family tree and write under everyone: This is my grandma. She has got a garden.

Exit task: назви 5 членів сім'ї за 20 секунд.
