Meet my squad!

A post by Sophie, 13

Hi, I'm Sophie and I'm from Bristol. I'm in Year 8. This is my squad!

Dan is my best friend. He's funny and he's really good at football. He isn't good at maths, but that's OK, I am!

Mia and Kate are twins. They're 14. They aren't in my class, but we're in the same dance club.

And this is Biscuit. Biscuit is our cat. She's orange and she's very lazy. She isn't a fan of dogs. She's a fan of the sofa.

We aren't perfect, but we're a great team!

True or False?

1Sophie is from Manchester.
2Dan is good at football.
3Dan is good at maths.
4Mia and Kate are in Sophie's class.
5Biscuit is orange.
6Biscuit is a fan of dogs.

Переклад: Привіт, я Софі, я з Брістоля, вчуся у 8 класі. Ден мій найкращий друг, він смішний і добре грає у футбол, але не дуже в математиці. Мія й Кейт близнючки, їм 14, вони не в моєму класі, але ми в одному танцювальному гуртку. Біскіт наша кішка: руда, дуже лінива, не любить собак, любить диван. Ми не ідеальні, але ми класна команда!
Відповіді: F, T, F, F, T, F.

4The rule: am, is, are grammarteacher + student

To be means «бути / быть». In our language we often skip it («Я голодний»), but in English the sentence doesn't work without it: I am hungry.

I

am = 'm

I am tired.
I'm not late.

he / she / it

is = 's

My cat is lazy.
She isn't at home.

you / we / they

are = 're

We are friends.
They aren't bored.

Lifehack: am is like a cat that loves only one owner: only I. Is is for one, are is for many and for you. And amn't doesn't exist: only I'm not!

Як пояснити: на прикладі учня і його друзів: «You are a student. I am a teacher. Your friend is...». Правило коротке, не затримуйтесь, решту часу віддайте практиці. Попросіть одне речення з am, одне з is, одне з are.

5Am, is, are workout grammarteacher + student

A. Choose am, is or are

1I ___ a gamer.
2My cat ___ hungry.
3My friends ___ at the park.
4You ___ my favourite teacher.
5It ___ cold today.
6Mia and Kate ___ twins.

B. Write am, is or are

1I ___ in Year 7.
2My brother ___ 16.
3The cat ___ under the bed.
4We ___ in the same class.
5Maths ___ my favourite subject.
6Lucas and I ___ gamers.

C. Make it negative

1My phone ___ new.
2I ___ hungry, thanks.
3My parents ___ at home today.
4It ___ cold today.
5Biscuit ___ a dog.

D. Translate into English

1Я не втомлений.
2Мій кіт дуже смішний.
3Ми не в школі.
4Вона моя найкраща подруга.
5Вони з Лондона.
6Він не голодний.

Відповіді A: am · is · are · are · is · are. B: am · is · is · are · is · are. C: isn't · 'm not · aren't · isn't · isn't.
Звернути увагу: my cat, Dan, maths це «він / вона / воно», тому is. Lucas and I = we, тому are.

6Describe the picture speakingteacher + student

Choose a photo. Who are they? Where are they? How are they? Use is / are / isn't / aren't.

Help
  • They are friends. They are in the park / at home.
  • She is happy / funny / tired.
  • They aren't at school.
  • I think her name is ... She is ... years old.

Зразок до фото 1: «This is Emma and this is Grace. They are best friends. They are in the park. They aren't at school today. Emma is in a yellow top. They are happy!»
Навідні питання: How many people are there? Are they friends or sisters? Are they happy? Is it hot or cold? Where are they?
