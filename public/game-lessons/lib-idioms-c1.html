3Listening listeningteacher + student

Before you listen: a design team has received bad news from a client. What do you think the manager will say?

A team meeting

Your teacher reads the dialogue (or plays it). Listen first, then read.

Show the script

Manager: Right, let’s get the ball rolling. The client didn’t like our first design.

Emma: So it’s back to the drawing board?

Manager: I’m afraid so. And this time we can’t cut corners. They noticed the small mistakes.

Mark: To be fair, the deadline was crazy. Some of us really went the extra mile.

Manager: I know, and I appreciate it. But we need everyone to pull their weight this time.

Emma: Can we try something completely different? Let’s think outside the box.

Manager: Great. What about our new designer, Sam? Is he settling in?

Mark: He’s doing well, but it’s a steep learning curve for him. And he’s snowed under with the other project.

Manager: Then let’s not overload him. Mark, could you touch base with him on Wednesday?

Mark: Sure, no problem.

Manager: Send me your ideas by Friday. Once we send the new version, the ball is in their court.

Emma: Sounds good. Are we all on the same page?

Manager: I think so. It’s six o’clock, let’s call it a day.

Gist: listen once and choose.

1The team has to ___.
2At the end of the meeting, the team ___.

Відповіді: redo the design for the client · agrees on the next steps

Detail: listen again. True or False?

1The client noticed small mistakes in the first design.
2Mark thinks nobody worked hard on the first version.
3Sam is finding his new job easy.
4Mark will check in with Sam on Wednesday.
5The team must send their ideas by Monday.

Відповіді: True · False · False · True · False

Перше прослуховування: про що нарада. Друге: деталі. Потім попросіть учня виписати всі ідіоми, які він почув (їх 12), і пояснити 3 з них своїми словами. 13-15 хв.

4Language focus practiceteacher + student

Ідіоми живуть у реченні: дієслово змінюється за часом і особою (He never pulls his weight. / We cut corners last time. / I’ve been snowed under.), а займенник за контекстом (the ball is in your / their court). Не перевантажуй мову ідіомами: одна-дві за розмову звучать природно, п’ять поспіль звучать як підручник.

Відповіді: call it a day · their court · curve · page

Відповіді: The ball is in your court · Let’s call it a day · We’ll have to go back to the drawing board

Наголосіть, що ідіоми не можна перекладати дослівно і змінювати в них іменники (не their box, не learning line). Вправи 6-7 хв. Переклад може мати інші коректні варіанти: зараховуйте їх вручну.

5Speaking speakingteacher + student

Card A: the team lead. A project is behind schedule. Start the meeting, explain the problem, ask who can help and agree on the next steps. Use at least four idioms.

Card B: the team member. You are overloaded with another project and a colleague is not doing their part. Explain your situation honestly, suggest a solution and agree on when to check in.

Role play: run a 3-minute meeting using your cards, then swap roles.
Have you ever had to go back to the drawing board? What happened?
Is it ever OK to cut corners?
Who in your life always goes the extra mile?

Speaking: record a short story (1 minute).

Tell a story about a project at work or school that went wrong. Use at least three idioms from the lesson. (say it out loud)

Рольова гра 6-7 хв, потім питання. Рахуйте, скільки ідіом учень ужив доречно, і відзначте ті, що прозвучали неприродно. Voice-завдання можна перенести в домашнє.
